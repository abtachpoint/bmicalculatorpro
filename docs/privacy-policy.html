Privacy Policy for BMI Calculator Pro

Effective date: August 31, 2026

BMI Calculator Pro does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

Information We Collect

BMI Calculator Pro does not require an account and does not collect personal information, contact information, location data, payment information, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

Height, Weight and BMI Calculation

The height and weight values you enter are used only on your device to calculate your BMI. These values and your BMI result are not sent to the developer or to any server.

Third-Party Services

The current version of BMI Calculator Pro does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services.

Health Information Notice

BMI Calculator Pro is intended as a general adult screening utility. BMI is not a diagnosis and should not be treated as medical advice.

Data Sharing

Since the app does not collect personal data, no personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the developer contact information shown on the BMI Calculator Pro Google Play listing.
